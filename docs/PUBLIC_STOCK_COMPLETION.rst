Public-stock exploration: completion evidence
============================================

This is a requirement-to-evidence index, not a new experiment or a claim
that the goal is complete. The active work follows the requested preview-two,
choose-one-of-two and own-koi/both-yaku combination search. Earlier search
efficiency work and nine-profile rule transfer remain closed; their results
are in SEARCH_REUSE_RESULTS.rst, RESUMABLE_FRONTIER_RESULTS.rst,
PRUNED_OPTIMIZED_FRONTIER_RESULTS.rst and RULE_TRANSFER_RESULTS.rst.
No historical cohort is reopened by this index.

Evidence inspected before final evaluation
------------------------------------------

* **Rule coverage:** PUBLIC_STOCK_RULES.rst defines all eight combinations,
  including each factor alone and all three together. The V2 and V3 evidence
  ledgers each contain eight profiles, two parents and four computation
  budgets. The complete curves, rather than only the selected winner, are
  reported in PUBLIC_STOCK_RESULTS.rst and its referenced figures.
* **Implementation and observable information:** tests/test_public_stock.py
  covers complete Python/CPU/CUDA trajectories for every profile, known-card
  retention, observation-preserving hidden-world resampling, reset semantics
  and the stakes payment. Event e:35 records the original 45-test pass;
  this is historical validation, not a newly executed test run. Current V5C
  source identities still match all 43 frozen bindings. Evaluated decisions
  additionally receive independent Python replay under each study.
* **Adapted policies and multiple parents:** V2 evidence binds all sixteen
  adaptations and both control qualification results. V1's failed scratch
  qualification remains reported. Training completion, parameter checks and
  engineering smoke matches are not counted as strength measurements.
* **Independent confirmation:** public_stock_v2_evidence.json and
  public_stock_v3_evidence.json retain their separate selected-rule
  confirmations. Their full screen curves and confirmation sections were
  inspected. Selection data are not pooled with reserved data; different
  horizons, guards and world counts do not constitute a cache-only ablation.
* **Changed opponents and actual work:** public_stock_v4_evidence.json and
  PUBLIC_STOCK_WORK_ANALYSIS.rst retain all fourteen comparisons and twelve
  work checks. The failed full work criterion and unresolved ranking are
  completed results, not missing measurements to be replaced by a rerun.
* **Measured policy improvement:** public_stock_v5a_evidence.json retains all
  eighteen qualification endpoints and twelve contrasts. Both finalists
  qualify and control does not. Consequently V5B remains unexecuted; its
  launch condition is not relaxed. V5C is a separately specified comparison
  using new finalist actors and retained control, with unequal training
  budgets explicit.

Evidence ledger filenames above are relative to docs/paper/generated/.
Reported numerical outcomes and their limits remain in the original result
report and ledgers; this index does not duplicate or re-estimate them.

Completion does not require converting a negative criterion into a positive
one. It requires the specified observations, independent checks and complete
reporting. Nor does this finite family establish that no other local rule or
algorithm could improve play. Any subsequent research should address a new
question; it must not extend consumed deals or repeat a study solely to obtain
significance.
