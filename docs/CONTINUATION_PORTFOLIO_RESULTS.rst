Complete learned continuations: closed development result
========================================================

The nineteen-source development comparison is complete. Every one of its 38
saved programs passed independent Python prefix reconstruction, adopted-pair
terminal evaluation and actual-suffix replay. An independent reduction checked
all frozen inputs, program/selection/audit identities, weighted exact gains,
imported baselines, work totals and the predeclared selection criterion.

Scope and decision
------------------

The sources are the sixteen existing five-/six-draw fixtures plus the closed
fresh-comparison misses 133, 216 and 241. They are reused development data;
this study acquires no new matches. The first candidate family adds the other
frozen parent to one-action parent-default programs. The second also adds
assisted WP and Point. The original parent remains the reference. Limits are
two million transitions globally and 100,000 per local comparison, with a
250,000 prefix cap and two workers. Candidate order and allocation were frozen.

The exact summed increments are 13292351/3745942200 for the baseline,
975582683/389577988800 for the smaller family and 5029931/2996753760 for the
larger. These are sums of conditional terminal win-score increments, not
population win rates. Planning work increases by 3.5988% and 7.5134%.
The smaller family adopts 111 local programs and the larger 69, including
three WP and five Point continuations in the latter.

Each family adds two positive sources but loses fixture 15's prior tiny gain.
The smaller newly improves six-draw fixture 10 and closed-comparison source
241; the larger newly improves sources 133 and 241. Neither improves source
216. Thus complete learned continuations expand the set of improvable cases,
but spreading the same allowance over them loses more gain elsewhere.

Both families FAIL the frozen advancement criterion: a new positive source,
retention of all baseline positives, no smaller summed exact gain, and no
smaller gain per transition. Neither advances to fresh confirmation or
replaces the default. All realized own-point and terminal-win-score deltas
are zero. No training or rule change was performed.

Work and timing
---------------

Planning-time sums are 1796.545 seconds for the smaller and 1930.859 seconds
for the larger family. The imported baseline sum is 1737.693 seconds from
earlier runs. The smaller family always runs first within each source, two
workers run concurrently, and baseline timings are imported: these are
descriptive operational measurements, not an isolated latency comparison.

Each family uses 95,809 independent prefix-oracle transitions. Local exact
oracles use 2,105,562 transitions for the smaller family and 1,130,783 for the
larger. Each executes 178 actual-suffix transitions and separately replays
the same 178. Those oracle/replay branches are not independent source matches
and are not included in planning work.
