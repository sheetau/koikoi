\section{Executable improvement from public information}
\label{sec:incumbent-tail}
The recovered opponent model supports a direct alternative to learning a
response: evaluate complete endgame policies under the hidden-card distribution
consistent with public play. We implement this approach at the first learner
decision in the final round with at most five draws remaining, provided a
preceding round has already identified the opponent. The retained parent plays
the entire prefix.

\subsection{From compatible histories to complete policies}
For each legal hidden hand, we replay the observed opponent actions with the
identified deterministic policy. Compatible hands and the remaining stock
permutations define a finite belief under a uniform valid-deal law. Integer
multiplicities represent equivalent unused stock suffixes. Chance branches
expand only when a draw occurs, and terminal branches contribute their weighted
outcomes immediately.

The planner groups worlds by the learner's own and public history before
choosing an action. Indistinguishable worlds therefore share that choice.
It returns a map from these histories to actions for the complete continuation,
along with an exact rational match value. Executing the map preserves the
information constraints used during evaluation.

We first evaluate root actions followed by the unchanged parent, then search
for policies that also optimize later decisions. Each phase receives a separate
cap of 500,000 live nodes and two million native transitions. An unresolved
branch can bound a candidate's value but cannot supply its executable policy.
When a cap interrupts search, previously completed policies remain available.
The controller adopts the best retained policy only if its exact value exceeds
the unchanged parent's value under the same belief. Otherwise it follows the
parent. A root-only control assigns the combined allowance to the first phase.

\subsection{The conditional improvement guarantee}
Let $H$ be the own/public history at the first eligible root, $\pi$ the parent,
and $\sigma_H$ an adopted complete policy. For final match score
$W\in\{0,\tfrac12,1\}$, the adoption test is
\[
  \Delta(H)
  = \mathbb{E}_{\nu}[W(\sigma_H)\mid H]
    -\mathbb{E}_{\nu}[W(\pi)\mid H] > 0.
\]
Here $\nu$ specifies the deal law, fixed opponent and game rules, and the
evaluated belief is its conditional law given $H$. Set $\Delta(H)=0$ whenever
the controller retains the parent, including unavailable roots or an incomplete
parent evaluation. Because the controller and parent share their entire prefix,
the law of total expectation gives
\[
  \mathbb{E}_{\nu}[W(\mathrm{controller})]
  -\mathbb{E}_{\nu}[W(\pi)]
  =\mathbb{E}_{\nu}[\Delta(H)]\geq 0.
\]
This argument requires commitment to the evaluated continuation and the stated
conditional model. It needs neither a globally optimal policy nor a completed
comparison of every action. Its role is to connect a verified local comparison
to expected match performance.

\subsection{Verified opportunities in the development cohort}
In a closed exploratory cohort of 64 source matches on 16 shared deal streams,
the controller adopts five policies for the first parent and two for the
second. Averaged over all 32 sources per parent, including missing roots and
fallbacks, exact conditional gains are 0.7969 and 0.5905 percentage points.
Every realized win outcome on these source continuations is unchanged.

One retained policy keeps the parent's first action while improving its later
decisions. Its exact value rises from $1711/2640$ to $55113/80080$, a gain of
$4819/120120$ (4.0118 percentage points) at that root. Search reaches its cap,
but this policy and its parent reference are complete. Independent Python-rule
replay verifies both values, and actual execution checks every selected action
against the policy's own/public-history key.

These witnesses establish usable conditional improvements under the declared
belief. Their frequency and realized match benefit are separate empirical
quantities. The uniform valid-deal belief is also distinct from a posterior over
the simulator's finite PRNG seeds. The implementation, certificates and full
work accounting are recorded in \texttt{docs/INCUMBENT\_RECOVERY\_RESULTS.rst}.

\subsection{Prospective comparison under a fixed allowance}
We next fixed 512 source matches on 128 new deal streams, crossing both parents,
both published opponents and both seats. The source matches follow the parent
through the activation point. At each eligible root, the parent, root-only
control and contingent controller execute from the same saved state. Missing
roots and budget refusals retain the parent and remain in the denominator.
Both search arms have a maximum allowance of four million native transitions
and one million live nodes per root, with fresh actor caches.

The controller adopts 41 and 42 policies for the two parents, compared with
28 each for root-only search. Its mean conditional gains over the parents are
0.2619 and 0.3246 percentage points across all 256 sources per parent.
Table~\ref{tab:prospective-incumbent} reports both these values and the realized
win-score differences. Those differences are +0.3906 and 0.0000 points.
The controller changes one realized score for the first parent and four for
the second; the latter changes cancel in the mean.

The primary criterion required positive lower bounds for all four actual-score
contrasts. We average the two seats within each deal, then resample 128 deal
clusters within opponent using 20,000 bootstrap replicates. Bonferroni-adjusted
percentile intervals provide the nominal simultaneous 95\% comparison.
The criterion is not met. A zero-width interval for an all-zero contrast
describes this finite sample and does not establish population equivalence.

Contingent planning uses 227.61 million physical transitions, versus
150.69 million for root-only search, and reaches a cap in 53 versus 16 phases.
The shared maximum allowance therefore does not imply equal realized work.
Independent Python replay verifies every adopted parent/candidate value pair;
separate trace replay checks all actual continuations. The complete report is
\path{docs/PROSPECTIVE_INCUMBENT_RESULTS.rst}.
This comparison establishes frequent executable conditional improvements at
the five-draw gate, while the realized results do not establish an additional
replicated match-win gain.
