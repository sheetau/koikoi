\section{Public-frontier bounds and development results}
\label{app:frontier}
\subsection{A public bound on prefix construction}
At the original gate, five draws remain when the learner acts either after
turn 10's stock draw or before turn 11's stock draw. Thirteen stock cards remain.
The opponent holds three cards in the first case and two in the second.
The learner's next discard is reached after at most one or two new draws,
respectively, unless the round terminates first.

There are at most $\binom{16}{3}=560$ or $\binom{15}{2}=105$ compatible
opponent hands. History consistency and valid-deal constraints can reduce
these counts. With a single deterministic opponent model, each hand and
ordered draw prefix has one parent path. Thus the numbers of prefix rows
are bounded by $560\cdot13=7{,}280$ and
$105\cdot13\cdot12=16{,}380$. A turn has at most four decisions; forced
steps can reduce this number. Table~\ref{tab:frontier-bound} multiplies the
maximum path length by the row bound to bound physical transitions.

The prefix budget is 250,000 transitions and 65,536 rows. All four entry phases
therefore fit without increasing either limit. The complete partition includes
terminal mass and all first-arrival beliefs. Tests cover both learner seats,
all entry phases, hidden-card reassignment and independent Python reconstruction.
The bound applies to the identified single-model gate; it does not extend to
an arbitrary root or a mixture of opponent models.

\subsection{Local budgets and independent verification}
Frontier histories are attempted in descending arrival mass, with exact public
history as the deterministic tie-break. Each attempt receives equal proposal
and reference allowances, capped at 50,000 transitions each and at half the
remaining shared allowance. Local node/task limits are 25,000 per phase.
Unused transitions remain available to later histories. Prefix work and failed
attempts count toward the same two-million-transition cap.

A proposal is usable only when it defines a complete public program, including
its parent-default regions. The reference is the unchanged parent everywhere.
The independent verifier rebuilds all frontier posteriors using Python rules
and fresh actor calls, then executes both policies through every remaining
chance branch for each adopted local program. It does not substitute the
search certificates for terminal outcomes. A small full-root fixture also
checks that the complete policy-value difference equals the sum of local gains.

\subsection{Complete incremental population evaluation}
The fixed study contains \FrontierIncrementSources{} sources, 1,024 per
parent--opponent population. Its contrast is the frontier extension minus
the base controller. A source contributes the exact conditional increment
from the complete local-value decomposition, or zero for identical policies.
The primary criterion requires positive simultaneous lower bounds for both
fixed equal-opponent mixtures. This is distinct from the closed
base-controller study's 0.1-point practical criterion.

The threshold construction of Appendix~\ref{app:uniform-law} uses the following fixed grid in win-score units:
\[
\{10^{-6},10^{-5},10^{-4},10^{-3},10^{-2},10^{-1},1/2\}.
\]
The family error $1/20$ is split across four populations and seven thresholds.
Exact binomial tail inversion gives downward-rounded 32-bit probability
bounds. Integrating the resulting lower step function yields each population
mean bound; averaging the two relevant bounds gives the fixed-mixture bound.
All 256 chunks passed independent source, posterior, program and value audits
before inference. Complete-source re-reduction agrees with the saved summary.

\paragraph{Acquisition amendment.}
An entry-scope error was corrected after 15 sources: forced steps can skip the
five-draw decision, while the source gate includes shorter tails. Corrected
dispatch keeps the base controller there. All 15 sources remain byte-for-byte
at their original indices; the other 4,081 were sampled after the correction.
No source was discarded or resampled, and the original denominator, selected
five-draw method, budgets and statistical criterion were retained. The failed
run and amendment are recorded in
\path{docs/FRONTIER_INCREMENTAL_SCOPE_AMENDMENT.rst}.

Of 4,096 sources, 2,153 have no eligible root and 1,943 reach evaluation.
The base controller has 626 strict-gain selections, 1,257 no-gain decisions
and 60 unresolved parent references. The extension preserves the first two
classes and improves 26 of the unresolved-reference cases. The complete
population table is Table~\ref{tab:frontier-increment}. Both primary mixture
bounds and all four secondary population bounds are positive. Actual execution
and independent replay give zero realized score difference in all populations
(Table~\ref{tab:frontier-actual}); these descriptive outcomes are separate from
the conditional-expectation inference.

\paragraph{Computational work.}
The base controller executes 901,397,514 physical transitions in its fixed
phase and 1,013,583,010 in its optimized phase. Additional prefix, proposal and local-reference work
uses 370,024, 92,499,292 and 19,004,982 transitions: 111,874,298 in total.
This is increased actual work within the existing maximum allowance, not
a latency improvement. Independent prefix verification executes 370,024
transitions, representing 1,054,701,450 weighted decisions. Full local-value
verification executes 12,067,877 transitions, representing 79,200,636 decisions.
Actual suffix execution and recorded-action replay each use 14,730 transitions
over 3,886 completions. These counts do not add source matches.

Source acquisition comprises 475,740 decisions and 31,819 round endings over
4,096 matches. Of these, 1,735 decisions, 118 rounds and 15 matches are retained
from the failed run; 474,005 decisions, 31,701 rounds and 4,081 matches are new.
The failed run separately records 1,649,050 completed planning transitions;
one interrupted worker's unsaved partial planning and suffix work are unknown.
The complete machine-readable ledger
\path{docs/paper/generated/frontier_increment_results_evidence.json}
retains acquisition, native execution, sampling, support reconstruction,
planning and audit work separately, together with every threshold count and
the exact rational effects and bounds.
